Job Title: Machine Learning Engineer

Responsibilities:
- Design and develop machine learning models for production.
- Build and optimize data pipelines for training and inference.
- Collaborate with data scientists and software engineers.
- Ensure scalability and efficiency of ML solutions.

Required Skills:
- Strong proficiency in Python.
- Experience with TensorFlow or Keras.
- Knowledge of NLP techniques and libraries.
- SQL and database management.
- Familiarity with Git and version control.

Education:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s in Computer Science, Data Science, or related field.
