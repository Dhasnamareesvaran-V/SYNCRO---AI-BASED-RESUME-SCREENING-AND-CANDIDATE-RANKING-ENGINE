Job Title: Data Analyst

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean, and analyze large datasets.
- Build dashboards and reports for business stakeholders.
- Provide insights to support decision-making.
- Work closely with cross-functional teams.

Required Skills:
- Advanced Excel skills.
- Experience with Tableau or Power BI.
- Strong knowledge of SQL.
- Understanding of statistics and data visualization.
- Problem-solving and communication skills.

Education:
- Bachelor’s in Statistics, Economics, Data Science, or related field.
